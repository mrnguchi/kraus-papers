# Printing paper product images

The product cards use the supplied files listed below. Filenames and capitalization in `app/data/products.php` match the files in this folder, including on case-sensitive hosting.

## Office & Copy Paper

- `White A4 Copy Paper.png` — White A4 Copy Paper
- `A4 Copy Paper.png` — A4 Copy Paper (red-and-white packaging reference)
- `A4 Paper for Commercial and Office Use.png` — A4 Paper for Commercial and Office Use
- `Everyday Office Copy Paper.png` — Everyday Office Copy Paper

## Large-Size Flat Paper

- `Large-Size Flat Paper.png` — Large-Size Flat Paper
- `Single-Side PE-Coated Flat Paper.png` — Single-Side PE-Coated Flat Paper
- `White Kraft Paper Sheets.png` — White Kraft Paper Sheets
- `Double-Sided Cardboard.png` — Double-Sided Cardboard
- `Glossy Cardboard Sheets.png` — Glossy Cardboard Sheets

## Coloured Paper

- `Coloured Craft and Copy Paper.png` — Coloured Craft and Copy Paper
- `Coloured A4 Printing Pape.png` — Coloured A4 Printing Paper

If a supplied image uses another filename or format, update its matching `image` path in `app/data/products.php`.
